Shipping

We plan to ship within Australia. Shipping costs and delivery times will be confirmed once our printing and fulfilment partner is chosen, and will be shown at checkout before you pay.

  • Custom and made-to-order items are printed after you order, so they take longer than stock items.
  • You’ll get an email with tracking once your order is on its way.
  • International shipping: to be confirmed.
  • Make sure your delivery address is correct. Contact us quickly if it needs changing.
DRAFT – to be reviewed

Returns and refunds

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law aren’t affected by this policy. If an item is faulty, not as described or doesn’t match what you ordered, you’re entitled to a repair, replacement or refund.

  • Change of mind on standard items: return window and conditions to be confirmed.
  • Custom and personalised items (including Your Truck prints) are made to order and generally can’t be returned for change of mind, but this doesn’t limit your Australian Consumer Law rights.
  • To start a return, contact us with your order number and photos of any fault.

Privacy

We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

  • What we collect: the details you send us through our forms (name, email, phone, message) and any photos you upload for a custom print.
  • Why: to reply to you, prepare a custom design or quote, and fill orders once ordering opens.
  • Who sees it: our team and the service providers we use to run the site, process forms and payments, and print and deliver orders. We don’t sell your information.
  • Your photos: used only for your request unless you give us permission to share them.
  • Access and correction: contact us to see, update or delete your information.

FAQ

Can I buy something now?

Not yet. This is a preview of the storefront. Orders aren’t open and no payments are taken.

How much will things cost?

Prices shown are provisional and pending costs. T-shirts are $45 for one, or $40 each when you buy two or more. Hoodie and other garment prices are still to be confirmed. The custom photo option is about +$7.50, also to be confirmed. Final prices will include GST.

What sizes will there be?

We’re planning S to 3XL. A size guide will be added once the garments are chosen.

Can I get my own truck on a shirt?

Yes, that’s the idea. Send a request through Your Truck and we’ll be in touch.

Do you do uniforms or bulk orders?

Custom truck artwork and uniforms are on the list. Get in touch to talk about it.
