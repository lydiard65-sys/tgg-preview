Truckin’ Good Gear

Our story.

From the cab.
For the crew.

Truckin’ Good Gear is Australian truck-themed apparel and merch for truckies and truck enthusiasts. Born from life behind the wheel, it’s about the trucks we drive, the work we do and the mates who make the miles better.

The idea is simple: gear built for the long haul. Recognisable Aussie trucks, straight-up slogans that sound like the people wearing them, and a small TGG tag to finish the garment. Proudly Australian, practical and with a sense of humour.

We’re starting with tees and building out to hoodies, polos, hi-vis, caps and beanies, stickers, mud flaps and mugs, plus custom prints of your own truck.
